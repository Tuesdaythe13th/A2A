### 3. Research Proposal (400 words max)

**Title:** Defensive Acceleration for Cognitive Privacy: Adversarial Robustness Against Behavioral Inference Attacks

**Problem Statement:**
Modern AI systems extract sensitive cognitive information from behavioral metadata with alarming accuracy. Academic research demonstrates that mouse dynamics alone can reveal stress levels, neurodivergence, mental health conditions, and decision-making patterns with >85% accuracy. Unlike traditional biometrics (fingerprints, face ID), these "cognitive biometrics" remain unprotected by privacy legislation and can be collected without consent through standard web analytics.

This creates asymmetric power: surveillance capabilities scale faster than defensive measures. Vulnerable populations—neurodivergent individuals, domestic violence survivors, political dissidents—face disproportionate risks from automated profiling systems.

**Research Questions:**
1. **Robustness Evaluation:** How do we measure an AI system's resilience to adversarial behavioral inputs across diverse cognitive profiles?
2. **Defensive Mechanisms:** Can we develop "cognitive camouflage" that preserves user functionality while degrading profiling accuracy?
3. **Standardization:** What benchmarks are needed to evaluate behavioral inference systems against adversarial robustness criteria?

**Proposed Approach:**
Building on my Cognitive Canary framework, I propose:

1. **Benchmark Development:** Extend MLCommons AILuminate with a "Cognitive Privacy" hazard category evaluating resistance to behavioral inference attacks. Develop standardized test suites measuring model degradation under adversarial conditions.

2. **Defensive Tools:** Create production-ready implementations of:
   - Lissajous-kinematic noise injection for mouse dynamics
   - Typing cadence obfuscation preserving natural feel
   - Zero-knowledge proofs for "humanity verification" without biometric exposure

3. **Vulnerability Assessment:** Audit commercial behavioral analytics platforms (DataDome, PerimeterX, BioCatch) to identify exploitation vectors and measure baseline profiling capabilities.

4. **Open Safety Standards:** Collaborate with Constellation researchers to establish "Cognitive Sovereignty" as a measurable safety property, with proposed metrics for:
   - Inference entropy (model confusion under defensive noise)
   - Functionality preservation (user experience impact)
   - Demographic fairness (protection across neurodivergent/neurotypical populations)

**Expected Impact:**
This research will produce:
- First comprehensive benchmark for behavioral inference robustness
- Open-source defensive toolkit deployed via browser extensions and accessibility tools
- Policy recommendations for regulating cognitive biometric collection
- Academic publications establishing "cognitive privacy" as a core AI safety primitive

The work advances adversarial robustness beyond traditional adversarial ML by focusing on **defensive rights** rather than offensive capabilities, ensuring AI systems remain robust against misuse while empowering users with cognitive self-determination.
