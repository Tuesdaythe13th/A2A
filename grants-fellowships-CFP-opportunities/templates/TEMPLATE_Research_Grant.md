## 🎯 REVIEW CRITERIA

### Scored Criteria (9-point scale, 1=exceptional, 9=poor)

1. **Significance** (scored)
   - Does the project address an important problem or critical barrier?
   - If successful, how will scientific knowledge, technical capability, and/or clinical practice be improved?
   - How will completion advance the field?

2. **Investigator(s)** (scored)
   - Are the PIs, collaborators, and other researchers well suited to the project?
   - Do they have appropriate experience and training?
   - If Early Stage Investigator or New Investigator, do they have appropriate experience?
   - If established, have they demonstrated ongoing research achievement?

3. **Innovation** (scored)
   - Does the application challenge and seek to shift current paradigms?
   - Are the concepts, approaches, methodologies, instrumentation, or interventions novel?
   - Are refinements, improvements, or new applications proposed?

4. **Approach** (scored)
   - Are the overall strategy, methodology, and analyses well-reasoned and appropriate?
   - Are potential problems, alternative strategies, and benchmarks for success presented?
   - If the project is in early stages, are the plans to establish feasibility described?
   - Are the preliminary studies (if applicable) strong?
   - Is the statistical plan rigorous?
   - Are authentication methods for key resources described?

5. **Environment** (scored)
   - Will the scientific environment contribute to the probability of success?
   - Are the institutional support, equipment, and other resources available or accessible?
   - Will the project benefit from unique features of the environment, subject populations, or collaborative arrangements?

## ✅ APPLICATION CHECKLIST

- [ ] Assemble research team (PI, Co-Is, collaborators)

- [ ] Begin collecting preliminary data (if needed)
- [ ] Identify consultants and request commitment letters

- [ ] Draft Research Strategy (Significance, Innovation, Approach)
- [ ] Develop figures and tables
- [ ] Request biosketches from co-investigators and key personnel
- [ ] Request "Other Support" documents from all senior/key personnel
- [ ] Begin budget development (consult with pre-award office)

- [ ] Request letters of support from collaborators, resource providers

### After Review (~1 Week After Review Meeting)
- [ ] Check eRA Commons for summary statement (critique)

- [ ] Celebrate! 🎉

- [ ] Set up project accounts with institutional grants office

- [ ] Order equipment and supplies
- [ ] Begin research!

- [ ] Meet with co-investigators to review summary statement
- [ ] Decide: Resubmit (A1) or submit as new application?
- [ ] Contact Program Officer for feedback and advice
- [ ] Develop plan to strengthen application:
  - Address weaknesses noted by reviewers
  - Collect additional preliminary data
  - Strengthen team (add consultants/collaborators)
  - Clarify approach or significance
- [ ] If resubmitting: Prepare 1-page "Introduction to Resubmission"
- [ ] If submitting as new: Revise and resubmit to next cycle
