## 💰 PRIZE STRUCTURE

### Stage 1: Non-Cash Prize
- **Winners**: Up to 12 teams
- **Prize**: 6-month biomedical product development mentorship
- **Value**: Dedicated one-on-one training from NIDA entrepreneurship experts
- **Activities**:
  - Five monthly 90-minute lectures
  - Five monthly 60-90 minute office hours with assigned mentor
  - Five monthly homework assignments
  - **Time Commitment**: ~25 hours per month

### Stage 2: Cash Prizes
- **Eligibility**: Teams that successfully complete Stage 1 mentorship
- **Prize**: Up to $10,000 per team (cash)
- **Total Prize Purse**: Up to $100,000 across all winning teams
- **Requirement**: Deliver team startup presentation

**Expected Use of Prize Money**:
- Develop minimum viable product (MVP)
- Obtain customer feedback
- Assess feasibility of science-based small business creation
- Develop key concepts for NIDA SBIR/STTR application

## 💡 ARTIST WELLBEING RESEARCH ALIGNMENT

### Alignment Score: **10/10** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

### Why This Is a Perfect Fit

**Problem Statement**:
Creative professionals (musicians, artists, photographers, etc.) experience:
- Higher rates of substance use disorder (SUD)
- Economic displacement from streaming collapse, AI, pandemic
- Lack of health insurance and access to treatment
- Chronic financial stress → mental health decline → substance use
- Social isolation from gig economy work
- No existing screening or intervention tools designed for this population

**Opportunity**: First-in-class digital health intervention for creative workforce SUD prevention and early intervention.

---

## 🚀 POTENTIAL STARTUP CONCEPTS

### Option 1: **CreativeCompass** - Digital Mental Health & SUD Prevention Platform
**Target Customers**: Working musicians, artists, photographers (non-famous creatives)

**Product**:
- Mobile app + web dashboard
- Economic Displacement Index (EDI) tracking (streaming revenue, gig frequency, AI threats)
- Validated screening: Economic Stress Resilience Scale (ESRS), AI Displacement Anxiety Scale
- Real-time SUD risk prediction based on financial volatility
- Peer support matching (genre-specific, career stage-matched)
- Resource navigation (low-cost treatment, artist health insurance, emergency funds)
- Crisis intervention pathways

**Business Model**:
- B2B: Arts organizations, musician unions, streaming platforms pay for member access
- B2C: Freemium model, premium features for subscribers
- B2G: State arts councils, SAMHSA-funded programs

**MVP (with $10K)**:
- Basic app with EDI tracking and screening tools
- Pilot with 50-100 artists across 3 genres
- Validate predictive model (economic stress → SUD risk)
- User feedback on features and usability

**SBIR/STTR Potential**:
- Phase I: Validate screening accuracy, refine risk algorithm
- Phase II: RCT of intervention effectiveness, expand to 5,000 users
- Commercialization: Scale to 100K+ creative professionals

---

### Option 2: **ArtistRisk Screener** - SUD Risk Assessment Tool
**Target Customers**: Treatment providers, artist health clinics, arts organizations

**Product**:
- Evidence-based screening tool specifically calibrated for creative professionals
- Integrates novel risk factors: Economic displacement, career stage, genre-specific stressors
- Quick assessment (5-10 minutes)
- Automated referral pathways
- Longitudinal tracking and outcomes monitoring

**Business Model**:
- SaaS licensing to clinics and treatment centers
- Per-screening fee model for organizations
- Integration with EHR systems

**MVP (with $10K)**:
- Validate screening instrument with 200 creative professionals
- Compare to gold-standard SUD assessments
- Test with 5 artist health clinics
- Develop clinical decision support algorithm

**SBIR/STTR Potential**:
- Phase I: Psychometric validation, sensitivity/specificity analysis
- Phase II: Implementation study across 50 clinics, outcomes research
- Commercialization: National rollout to treatment networks

---

### Option 3: **GigGuard** - Gig Economy Worker SUD Surveillance Dashboard
**Target Customers**: Public health agencies, researchers, arts advocacy organizations

**Product**:
- Real-time surveillance system for SUD risk in creative gig workers
- Integrates: Streaming data, copyright registrations, social media sentiment, public health data
- Early warning system for emerging crises (e.g., venue closures, AI displacement)
- Geographic hotspot identification
- Evidence base for policy interventions

**Business Model**:
- Grant-funded (SAMHSA, CDC, HRSA)
- Subscription for state health departments and arts councils
- Open data API for researchers (with privacy protections)

**MVP (with $10K)**:
- Proof-of-concept dashboard for 3 states
- Data integration from 2-3 sources (streaming + public health)
- Validation: Does dashboard predict county-level SUD rates in artists?
- User testing with 5 public health officials

**SBIR/STTR Potential**:
- Phase I: Develop predictive models, validate dashboard utility
- Phase II: National expansion, intervention linkage, policy impact study
- Commercialization: Nationwide surveillance network

---

### Option 4: **HarmonyHub** - Peer Support Network for Musicians in Recovery
**Target Customers**: Musicians with SUD, in treatment, or in recovery

**Product**:
- Peer-to-peer support platform (app + web)
- Genre-specific matching (e.g., hip hop artists with hip hop artists)
- Career stage matching (emerging vs established)
- Anonymous support groups
- Sober touring resources (venues, lodging, peer networks)
- Financial coaching for artists in recovery
- Integration with treatment providers

**Business Model**:
- Free for users (mission-driven)
- Funded by: Arts foundations, recovery organizations, musician unions
- Premium features: Career coaching, financial planning

**MVP (with $10K)**:
- Beta platform with 50 users across 5 genres
- Test matching algorithm effectiveness
- Measure engagement and user satisfaction
- Pilot sober touring resource database

**SBIR/STTR Potential**:
- Phase I: Test peer support efficacy, engagement metrics
- Phase II: RCT comparing peer support + treatment vs treatment alone
- Commercialization: Expand to all creative disciplines, national reach

## ✅ APPLICATION STRATEGY

### Strengths to Highlight
- ✅ **Novel Population**: First digital health tool for non-famous creative professionals
- ✅ **Evidence-Based**: Grounded in longitudinal research (artist wellbeing study)
- ✅ **Clear Need**: High SUD rates + economic displacement + no existing solutions
- ✅ **Innovative Risk Factors**: Economic Displacement Index, AI threat, streaming collapse
- ✅ **Measurable Outcomes**: SUD risk reduction, treatment uptake, recovery support
- ✅ **SBIR/STTR Pathway**: Clear plan for Phase I → Phase II → commercialization
- ✅ **Experienced Team**: Research expertise + domain knowledge + entrepreneurial drive

### Competitive Advantages
- **Underserved Market**: No existing SUD tools designed for creative professionals
- **Data Integration**: Novel combination of economic + health + industry data
- **Genre Specificity**: Hip hop artists have different needs than classical musicians
- **Real-Time Risk**: Economic displacement → SUD prediction (not retrospective)
- **Community Trust**: Developed BY artists FOR artists

### Potential Weaknesses to Address
- ⚠️ **Commercialization Risk**: Will artists pay for digital health tools?
  - **Mitigation**: B2B model (arts orgs pay), free tier for users
- ⚠️ **Stigma**: Will artists use SUD-related tools?
  - **Mitigation**: Framed as economic wellbeing + career support first
- ⚠️ **Fragmented Market**: Artists work across many genres/disciplines
  - **Mitigation**: Platform approach, genre-specific modules
- ⚠️ **Privacy Concerns**: Sensitive financial + health data
  - **Mitigation**: HIPAA compliance, de-identified data, user control
